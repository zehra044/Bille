
# Bille

## Version

0.1 (Draft)

---

# Product Vision

## Overview

Bille is a mobile application that helps individuals and businesses collect money from customers by tracking charges, payments, and remaining balances.

The application provides a simple and reliable way to manage customer accounts without using notebooks or spreadsheets.

---

# Problem Statement

Many people collect money from customers over time.

Customers often make partial payments instead of paying the full amount at once.

Using notebooks or memory makes it difficult to know:

- How much the customer owes
- How much has already been paid
- Remaining balance
- Payment history
- Deleted records
- Collection reports

This leads to mistakes, confusion, and lost information.

---

# Solution

Bille keeps a complete financial history for every customer.

Users can:

- Create customers
- Add charges
- Record payments
- Automatically calculate balances
- View transaction history
- Restore deleted transactions
- Generate reports
- Share customer statements
- Backup data

---

# Target Users

Bille is designed for anyone who collects money from customers.

Examples include:

- Small businesses
- Shop owners
- Contractors
- Freelancers
- Service providers
- Individual collectors

---

# Goals

The primary goals of Bille are:

- Make payment tracking simple
- Eliminate manual calculations
- Never lose transaction history
- Reduce mistakes
- Make customer information easy to access
- Allow quick sharing of customer statements

---

# Out of Scope (Version 1)

The following features are NOT included in the first version:

- Inventory management
- Product management
- Point of Sale (POS)
- Expense tracking
- Employee management
- Accounting
- Online payments

These may be considered in future versions.

---

# Success Criteria

Bille is successful if a user can:

1. Create a customer.
2. Add a charge.
3. Record a payment.
4. Instantly see the remaining balance.
5. View the complete transaction history.
6. Share the customer's statement.
7. Restore deleted transactions.
8. Backup and restore all data.# Bille
